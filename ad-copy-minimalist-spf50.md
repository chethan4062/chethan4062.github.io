## Claims to verify
- **"Buy 2, Get the 3rd Free":** confirm which products it covers, which item is free, and the end date. Pause offer lines when the code expires (ASCI expects price accuracy).
- **"Lab-tested at SPF 56.6":** supported by the product page (ISO 24444:2019). Keep the report handy in case Meta or ASCI asks for it.
- **"No white cast":** claimed on the page, but some reviews mention a slight cast on some skin tones. Test the demo creative across skin tones before scaling.
- **"Moisturiser-like":** a texture claim from the page. Avoid implying it replaces a moisturiser.
- The product isn't recommended during pregnancy or lactation, so exclude those audiences.
- "Repairs skin" from the page was left out on purpose because it reads as a treatment claim.

## What to test first
- **Proof vs objection (Meta primary text 2 vs 3):** shows whether this audience buys on lab credibility or on sensory reassurance.
- **Hook test (Script 1 body with Hook 2 vs Hook 3):** shows whether a number or a live demo stops the scroll better. Judge on 3-second view rate.
- **Offer vs no offer (Meta headline 4 vs 2, same creative):** shows whether Buy 2 Get 1 lifts conversion rate enough to justify the lower margin, or whether proof alone converts.
