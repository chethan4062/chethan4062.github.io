Ad Performance Health Check

Upload a daily Meta Ads or Google Ads export to spot problems fast: CPA spikes, creative fatigue, tracking breaks, pacing issues, and clear winners and losers.

Your file is processed only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

How to export your data
  • Meta: In Ads Manager, open the Ads tab (ad level recommended), set Breakdown to By time > Day, choose your date range (at least the last 10 days), then Export > CSV.
  • Google Ads: Open the Ads report (or Ad groups / Campaigns), add Segment > Time > Day, choose at least the last 10 days, then Download > CSV.
  • Tip: an ad-level export gives the most detail; the tool rolls it up to ad set / ad group and campaign automatically.
