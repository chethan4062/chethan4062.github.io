How to prepare a fair comparison

Use the same acquisition period for both files. CRM totals must describe leads acquired in that period, with enough time to convert. Do not compare this month's spend with customers acquired from older leads.

Summarise your CRM by campaign before exporting. Qualified leads must include customers; customers must be a subset of qualified leads. Use unique people, not CRM activity counts. Duplicate summary rows will be added together, so avoid overlapping exports.

Campaign and platform values must match across files (ignoring case and extra spaces). Prefer campaign IDs in both campaign columns when names are reused. If you omit platform in both files, campaign keys must be unique across channels. Unmatched campaigns stay visible but are excluded from combined metrics.

Revenue is optional and must be attributable to these customers. Choosing a currency changes the display only; it does not convert values. This tool reports ad spend per customer, excluding sales, software and other acquisition costs.

Metric definitions and limits

Quality rate = qualified leads ÷ leads. Cost per lead = spend ÷ leads. Cost per qualified lead = spend ÷ qualified leads. Ad cost per customer = spend ÷ customers. Revenue / spend uses CRM revenue, not platform-attributed revenue. Overall costs use totals, not averages of campaign ratios.

A dash means the denominator is zero, data is missing, or campaigns could not be matched. Rankings describe the supplied data; they do not establish causality or predict results after budget changes. Low-volume campaigns and recent lead cohorts need more time before decisions.
