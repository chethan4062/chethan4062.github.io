Marketing Calculators

Quick calculators I use to sanity-check campaign performance.

Inputs
Results —
ROAS
—
How it is calculated: Revenue from Ads ÷ Ad Spend
Break-even ROAS
—
How it is calculated: 1 ÷ (Gross Margin ÷ 100)
Profit after Ad Spend
—
How it is calculated: Revenue × (Gross Margin ÷ 100) − Ad Spend
Target ROAS
—
How it is calculated: 1 ÷ ((Gross Margin − Target Profit Margin) ÷ 100)
Inputs
Results —
CAC
—
How it is calculated: Ad Spend ÷ New Customers
LTV (gross profit)
—
How it is calculated: Average Order Value × Expected Orders × (Gross Margin ÷ 100)
LTV:CAC
—
How it is calculated: LTV ÷ CAC
Payback
—
How it is calculated: CAC ÷ (Average Order Value × Gross Margin ÷ 100), as orders to recover CAC
A common rule of thumb is an LTV:CAC of around 3:1 or higher. —
Inputs
Results —
Expected Spend by Today
—
How it is calculated: Monthly Budget × (Days Passed ÷ Days in Month)
Pacing
—
How it is calculated: Spend So Far ÷ Expected Spend × 100
Projected Month-end Spend
—
How it is calculated: (Spend So Far ÷ Days Passed) × Days in Month
Remaining Budget
—
How it is calculated: Monthly Budget − Spend So Far
Required Daily Spend for Rest of Month
—
How it is calculated: Remaining Budget ÷ (Days in Month − Days Passed)

Estimates for planning only. Results depend on the accuracy of your inputs.
